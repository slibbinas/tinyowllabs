Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TinyVision ("the app") is a Wear OS app, with a phone companion, that brings your Hik-Connect video intercom to your watch: call alerts with the visitor's photo, opening the door, rejecting a call and live video from your door stations. It is developed by TinyOwl, the developer name of Viktoras Šidlauskas, an individual developer. The app is not made, endorsed or supported by Hikvision. "Hikvision" and "Hik-Connect" are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to say which intercoms the app works with.

In short: the app has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your data stays on your watch and phone, except what has to go to Hik-Connect or to your own intercom to show video and open the door.

The phone app reads notifications only from the Hik-Connect app. Notifications from all other apps are ignored and never read or stored.

Android may include the app's private storage, with the sign-in details, in your own device backup to your Google account, if device backup is turned on in your phone or watch settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect or share personal data with the developer or any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not record or store video.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, health data, microphone or camera.
  • It does not sell data. There is no data to sell.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state, change network state (watch): to reach Hik-Connect and to ask for Wi-Fi when needed.
  • Internet (phone): to carry live video from Hik-Connect or your intercom to the watch.
  • Notifications: to show a call alert on the watch and status messages on the phone.
  • Vibration (watch): to buzz when someone rings.
  • Notification access (phone, granted by you in system settings): to see Hik-Connect call notifications. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Notification access; the watch then no longer wakes up for calls.
  • Folder access (phone, chosen by you): to keep the backup of settings and door photos in the folder you pick.

Your control over the data

  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the watch or phone settings, removes everything it stored there, including the sign-in details.
  • The backup folder and the diagnostic files on your phone stay until you delete them.
  • Your Hik-Connect account is managed by Hikvision under their own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle data from children.
