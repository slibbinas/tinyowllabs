Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

TinyBuds ("the app") is an Android app that shows the battery of your Bluetooth earbuds in a home screen widget: the overall level and, for earbuds that report it, the left bud, the right bud and the case. It is developed by TinyOwl, the developer name of Viktoras Šidlauskas, an individual developer.

In short: the app has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing is ever sent to the developer. Everything the app reads and stores stays on your phone. The only online service it uses is Google Play, for the optional Pro purchase.

What the app handles

Android may also include the app's settings in your phone's own backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data for the developer, and it does not share your data with anyone.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • It does not sell data. There is no data to sell.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect, asked on first use): to see your paired earbuds, whether they are connected, and their battery levels.
  • Run at startup: to keep the widget up to date after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: used only by Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase, and by Android's background task scheduler. The app itself does not connect to any server.

Your control over the data

  • Removing a widget removes its settings.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the phone settings, removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • A Pro purchase stays with your Google account and is restored when you reinstall the app.
