Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

TinySpools ("the app") is an Android app for keeping track of your 3D printer filament: which brands, materials and colors you have, how much is left on each spool and on which shelf it is. It is developed by TinyOwl, the developer name of Viktoras Šidlauskas, an individual developer.

In short: the app has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone, except backup files that you choose to save to a folder of your choice. The app goes online only when you ask it to update its barcode database, and then only downloads a public file; nothing about you is sent.

What the app handles

DataWhyWhere it is keptWhere it is sent
Your filaments, spools, remaining weight and shelvesThis is what the app is forOn the phone onlyNot sent anywhere
Backup files (optional)To restore your data, for example on a new phoneIn the folder you pick in the Android file picker, for example on your device or in Google DriveOnly to that folder, through Android. The developer never receives them

It happens only when you tap “Update barcode database” in Settings or confirm the update after scanning an unknown barcode

If you pick a cloud folder such as Google Drive for backups, the files are stored by that service under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect or share personal data with the developer or any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not go online in the background, and it does not request location, contacts or microphone.
  • It does not sell data. There is no data to sell.

Permissions

  • Camera (asked when you first scan): only to read barcodes and QR codes. Images are processed on the phone and never saved or sent.
  • NFC: to write a spool link to your own NFC sticker and to open that spool when you touch it.
  • Internet: only to download the Open Filament Database barcode file from api.openfilamentdatabase.org, and only when you ask for it. That server, like any web server, sees the download request (your IP address) under its own policy; the app sends no other data.

Access to a backup folder is granted by you in the Android file picker and can be removed by choosing another folder or uninstalling the app.

Your control over the data

  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the phone settings, removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Backup files stay in the folder you chose until you delete them there.

Third-party content
