Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TinySpools ("the app") is an Android app for keeping track of your 3D printer filament: which brands, materials and colors you have, how much is left on each spool and on which shelf it is. It is developed by TinyOwl, the developer name of Viktoras Šidlauskas, an individual developer.

In short: the app has no server of its own, no internet access, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone, except backup files that you choose to save to a folder of your choice.

What the app handles

DataWhyWhere it is keptWhere it is sent
Your filaments, spools, remaining weight and shelvesThis is what the app is forOn the phone onlyNot sent anywhere
Backup files (optional)To restore your data, for example on a new phoneIn the folder you pick in the Android file picker, for example on your device or in Google DriveOnly to that folder, through Android. The developer never receives them
App settings (backup folder, auto backup, palette style)To remember your choicesOn the phone onlyNot sent anywhere

If you pick a cloud folder such as Google Drive for backups, the files are stored by that service under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect or share personal data with the developer or any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • It does not sell data. There is no data to sell.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. Access to a backup folder is granted by you in the Android file picker and can be removed by choosing another folder or uninstalling the app.

Your control over the data

  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the phone settings, removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Backup files stay in the folder you chose until you delete them there.

Third-party content

The built-in filament catalog comes from SpoolmanDB (MIT License). Brand names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are shown only to identify a filament's manufacturer. TinySpools is not affiliated with or endorsed by any filament manufacturer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle data from children.
