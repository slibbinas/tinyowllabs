Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

TinySpools ("the app") is an Android app for keeping track of your 3D printer filament: which brands, materials and colors you have, how much is left on each spool and on which shelf it is. It is developed by TinyOwl, the developer name of Viktoras Šidlauskas, an individual developer.

In short: the app has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing is ever sent to the developer. Everything you enter stays on your phone, except backup files that you choose to save to a folder of your choice. The app goes online only for two things: to download the public barcode database when you ask for it, and - only if you sign in to your Bambu Lab account - to read your printers' AMS from Bambu Lab's cloud.

If you pick a cloud folder such as Google Drive for backups, the files are stored by that service under its own privacy policy. Your Bambu Lab account and printer data on Bambu Lab's servers are handled by Bambu Lab under its own privacy policy; TinySpools only reads them.

Android may also include the app's data in your phone's own backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. The encrypted Bambu Lab sign-in cannot be used from such a backup, because its key never leaves the phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data for the developer, and it does not share your data with anyone. The only third party it talks to with your data is Bambu Lab, and only if you sign in to your Bambu Lab account.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not go online in the background, and it does not request location, contacts or microphone.
  • It does not sell data. There is no data to sell.

Permissions

  • Camera (asked when you first scan): only to read barcodes and QR codes. Images are processed on the phone and never saved or sent.
  • NFC: to write a spool link to your own NFC sticker and to open that spool when you touch it.
  • Internet: to download the Open Filament Database barcode file from api.openfilamentdatabase.org when you ask for it, and, if you sign in to a Bambu Lab account, to sign in to Bambu Lab and read your printers' AMS from its cloud as described above. These servers, like any web server, see the request (your IP address) under their own policies; the app sends no other data.

Access to a backup folder is granted by you in the Android file picker and can be removed by choosing another folder or uninstalling the app.

Your control over the data

  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in the phone settings, removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • Settings → Bambu Lab account → Sign out removes the Bambu Lab sign-in and printer list from the phone.
  • Backup files stay in the folder you chose until you delete them there.
