Things the phone does

In the app the camera and NFC do this. Here these buttons pretend.

The spool's RFID tag gives the material, color and weight. A filament you already have opens; a new one is offered with Add.

For the barcode on a filament box, tap Scan in the phone.

The real app keeps everything on your phone: no account, no server. Bambu Lab and other brand names and logos are trademarks of their owners; TinySpools is an independent app.
