In the app NFC does this. Here these buttons pretend, on whatever screen is open - even the home screen.

A Bambu Lab spool's RFID tag gives the material, color and weight. Your own sticker opens its spool, and during Check spools marks it found. An empty sticker can be written from a spool (Label → NFC tag).

For barcodes on filament boxes and QR labels, tap Scan in the phone.
