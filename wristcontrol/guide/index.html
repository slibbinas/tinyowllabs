WRIST CONTROL for Vallox

Fresh air at home,
right on your wrist.

A Wear OS app for Vallox heat recovery ventilation, with a phone app. See how fresh the air is, switch modes in two taps, and let the app tune the unit to the way your home breathes.

See your air

CO2, humidity and temperatures at a glance, coloured green, yellow or red.

Smart modes

Every mode you pick in the app sets its own fan speed and CO2 limit, and Optimize tunes Auto mode to your home.

Know the cost

Wrist Control for Vallox3
The app

Everything about your air, one swipe apart.

Swipe left and right between pages. Tap a page to act on it.

Sensors

How the air in your home is right now: fan speed, temperature, humidity and CO2, coloured by comfort. Tap to see the comfort scales.

Temperatures

The four air streams: extract air from the rooms, supply air to them, outdoor air coming in and exhaust air going out. Tap for charts: the last 24 hours, the last 7 days and last year.

Unit

Your unit's name, the speed of both fans, whether the heat exchanger is recovering heat or bypassed to cool, and the unit's own clock.

Filters

When the filters were changed and when the next change is due. After fitting new ones, tap Mark changed, then Confirm today: the date is saved in the unit.

Ventilation

The mode the unit runs now, with the time left in Boost or Custom (1:00 with an hour or more left, 59 min when less), and the fan speed of Home, Away and Boost. Tap Open to switch modes.

Mode

Pick a mode here, not on the unit's panel: the app also sets the values that belong to it. The running mode spins.

Settings

Where the numbers come from, how often they refresh, which alerts are on, the temperature range and which pages are shown.

On your watch face

Indoor, outdoor or supply air temperature, CO2, humidity, fan speed or days to the filter change on your watch face, updated without opening the app.

Home and away

At home the app talks to the unit over Wi-Fi. Away, it uses the Vallox cloud and your MyVallox account.

Alerts that don't nag

One notice when indoor air leaves the comfort range, with the same scale as in the app. One when filters are due. A new one only when it gets worse.

Your pages

In Semi-automatic, hold a page to reorder pages; hide them in Settings. Keep only what you look at. Ventilation and Settings always stay.

Sign in from the phone

No typing on the watch: the phone app sends your MyVallox login to the watch. Without a phone, tap Sign in on the watch. English, Suomi, Lietuvių, Polski.

The phone app

The same app on your phone, with one device in charge.

The phone app shows the same windows as the watch, on a panel of tiles: ventilation, sensors, temperatures, Optimize, statistics, filters and settings. It opens on the panel; tap a tile to open its window. Setup is in Settings. On the home screen: widgets for the air, statistics and charts (a day or 7 days, temperature or CO2).

Panel · Semi-automatic

Every tile is a window. The amber line on top says how fresh the numbers are.

Ventilation

The same modes as on the watch. The running mode spins.

Who controls the unit

In Settings: This phone or The watch, each with its version below.

One device controls the unit

It reads the unit in the background, runs the Optimized automation, sends notifications and writes to the unit. The other device shows the same pages and passes mode changes and Mark changed to it. Choose in Settings → Unit controlled by; with both devices in reach the switch takes about a second. When the phone controls, the top of the watch shows PHONE: the numbers come from the phone.

When the other device does not answer

If the watch controls but is away or switched off, Settings offers Control from this phone. The watch follows the phone when it comes back, and changes made on it meanwhile are dropped.

If the phone controls but does not answer, watch Settings offers Control from this watch. The phone follows the watch when it comes back.

What the other device gets

A reading made on the phone reaches the watch at once. Statistics are calculated only by the controlling device; the other one asks and receives the result. Charts are calculated by either device and shared, so both show the same. A device that only displays needs the account only for charts and Optimize. A change made on it either goes through or says so: not in range, or not confirmed yet.

Which one to choose

The watch on your wrist reads most regularly. A phone lying still may pause background reading for hours; Settings → Phone asleep shows it and how to allow reading.

Updates

The watch and phone apps are updated together. Settings shows both versions; the panel asks to update the watch when it is behind. The phone app asks by itself when Google Play has a new version.

Two ways to use it

Let the app run the air, or run it yourself.

On first start the phone app asks How do you want to use it? You can switch any time in Settings → Automation: tap a card.

Automatic · phone

One card with how fresh the air is, then Boost, Statistics and Settings. A mode other than Optimized or Boost is named on the card.

Automatic · watch

The air and a Boost button. The amber line says how fresh the numbers are and, if they stop updating, why. Swipe for Statistics and Settings.

Settings → Automation

Tap a card to switch. In Settings → Setup, Run setup again asks the questions anew, starting from your current choices.

Automatic: Set it up for me

A few short questions, about 2 minutes: your VALLOX account (a free MyVallox account, created at cloud.vallox.com), which device controls the unit, the week schedule if the unit follows one, what matters most (fresh air, balanced or quiet), how warm you like your home and when to tell you. Then the app runs the air for you and adjusts the plan every week. Each adjustment comes with a notice and Undo.

Without Pro, or with Keep the schedule, there is no goal or plan question: Auto mode or the schedule sets the fan. When the watch controls, it reads every 15-60 min, which uses more battery.

Semi-automatic: I'll set it up myself

All windows and settings: modes, the Optimize plan, sensors, charts, statistics, the temperature range, notifications and the refresh rate. Every change is yours to make.

Boost

One tap: an hour of Boost. While it runs, the button says Stop Boost and shows the minutes left, with a spinning fan. Tap it to stop: the unit goes back to the mode it ran before, with the same settings. A spinner turns until the unit confirms. On the watch, if a change does not go through, the reason stays in amber where you tapped: under the Boost button or under the mode in the list.

Pull down to refresh

On any page of the watch and on the phone panel. A large spinner turns until new numbers arrive; a watch that does not control the unit asks the phone. On the phone, Statistics and Temperatures are recalculated the same way, or by tapping the round refresh badge under the numbers. The watch windows have an Update button.

Smart modes · the heart of the app

Switch modes in the app, and every mode runs the way it should.

Your Vallox keeps one fan speed for both Away and Auto, and one CO2 limit (the point where it starts adding air) for every mode. The unit's panel switches only the mode, so the unit keeps whatever values were set last. The app switches the mode and sets that mode's own values, in the same tap.

How Auto decides

Two settings decide it. The Away speed is the lowest speed the fan runs at. The CO2 limit is the point where the unit starts adding air: above it, about 1 % of fan speed for every 10 ppm, up to the Boost speed.

A lower limit or a higher Away speed means fresher air and more fan. Finding the right pair for your home is what Optimize does.

Example: Away speed 45 %, CO2 limit 500 ppm, Boost 85 %.

Optimized · automation

Optimized adjusts itself while you live.

While Optimized is on, the app checks four rules after every reading. It never drives the fans directly: two rules only change the fan at rest (the Away speed Auto starts from), and the unit's own CO2 control keeps working between readings. The third rule only offers a Boost, the fourth opens or closes the bypass. The numbers were checked against a year of real history (2025-09-15 - 2026-09-14, 47,789 records every 10 min).

Why the fan at rest, not the mode

"Nobody home" does not switch to Away. It only lowers the speed Auto starts from. When people come back, nobody has to wait for the app: the unit sees CO2 rising and adds air by itself, and at the next reading the app restores the Optimized value. The same holds for "Dry air".

Boost and Custom

While a temporary mode runs, the automation changes nothing and waits until the unit is back in Optimized.

Week clock

The unit's week clock switches the mode at set hours, so it and Optimized cannot both run. Under the modes, Week clock on starts the clock and ends Optimized; Optimized on does the opposite. The window lists the clock's events, read at home over Wi-Fi. Wrist Control has no week clock of its own: Optimized follows what actually happens at home. In Automatic, while the clock is on, the phone panel has a card to switch it off.

The app looks when something happens.

The busy hours are learned for each unit from its own history (when Optimize or Statistics downloads it) and from the changes the app sees. Until there is enough data: 6-8 and 19-22 h. The default is a fixed 1 h; Adaptive, 20 min, 30 min or Only in app can be chosen in Settings → Data refresh; in Automatic it is always Adaptive. Adaptive drains the battery faster. With Only in app nothing runs in the background: no notifications and no automation. At night Android may group the readings.

What the app changes

On its own only these: the mode, the Away speed, the CO2 limit, the week clock switch, and while Optimized runs, the bypass lock and the supply air target of Auto mode. When you ask, it also writes the filter change date and corrects the unit's clock.

What it never changes

The Home and Boost speeds, humidity control, the heater and Custom settings. Optimize stays within a safe range (fan at rest 20-70 %, CO2 limit 500-1000 ppm), and Restore brings back the previous values. Leaving Optimized hands the bypass and the supply air target back as they were.

Without a connection

The app changes nothing, and the unit keeps running with the last settings. After 3 h without a new reading, the readings turn grey with the age of the last reading, complications show --, and the controlling device sends one notice. If the cloud stops accepting your password, it says so at once.

Temperature range

Settings → Temperature sets the range, by default 22.5-23 °C. Below it the bypass is closed and the heat exchanger keeps the heat; above it, with outdoors at least 2 °C cooler, outdoor air cools the home, except in the heating season (outdoor 24 h average below 12 °C). The automation keeps the supply air target low so the electric heater stays off; the Temperatures page shows Heater on or off. Each state lasts at least an hour, so small swings do not move the damper. Let Vallox decide in the Optimize window turns it off.

Planned

Per-unit thresholds: the "empty home" CO2 level and the humidity jump worked out for each unit from its own history - today they are one apartment's numbers. Summer cooling with the fan: more air when the night is cooler outside than inside, less on a hot day. Weather forecast - once there is summer experience.

Optimize

Settings made for your home, not for an average one.

Every home produces CO2 differently: how many people, how big the rooms, when you sleep and cook. Optimize reads your unit's last 14 days, learns how much fresh air your home needs at each time of day, and picks the Away speed and CO2 limit for Auto mode.

1 · Analyse

Tap Analyse. The app reads the unit's 14-day history: from the unit at home, from the Vallox cloud when away.

2 · Choose a goal

Clean air (about 620 ppm), Balance (about 700) or Quiet: less air, less fan.

3 · See the forecast

Morning and daytime CO2 and fan speed, now › after. Nothing changes until you tap Apply. If the plan changes nothing, it says no change.

4 · Check after a day

The app compares the forecast with the real morning. Restore brings back your old values.

How it works

  • Learns your home. For the morning (6-10), day (10-18), evening (18-24) and night, it measures how fast your home adds CO2, from CO2 and fan speed in the history. It takes the middle of the daily values, so one unusual day - guests, windows shut - does not move the plan.
  • Tries every option. It simulates each Away speed from 20 to 70 % and CO2 limit from 500 to 1000 ppm and predicts where CO2 settles.
  • Picks the lowest total cost. Fan electricity (it grows with the cube of speed), heat carried out with the air in the heating season (the heat exchanger returns most of it; how much is measured from your unit's temperatures) and a penalty for every ppm above the goal. So a plan may let CO2 rise a little above the goal at peaks when that saves a lot of energy.
  • Shows the cost. The forecast has an Energy line against your current settings, and warns when a plan would use more than 1.5 times the energy: a lighter goal then uses less.
  • Goals, typical / busy hours: Clean 620 / 720 ppm · Balance 700 / 800 · Quiet 850 / 1000.
  • Stays safe. It never writes values outside those ranges, and keeps your previous values for Restore.
  • Apply counts only when the unit has taken the values: if writing fails, Apply stays and says why. If the refresh is not Adaptive, the app asks once whether to switch; Back there cancels Apply.
  • After Apply the window starts with Optimized and the goal, the values it wrote, plan and actual, then New plan and Restore. Restore shows only when there are earlier values to bring back. Below it is the temperature range of the Optimized automation.

Example home: Balance chose Away speed 40 % and CO2 limit 500 ppm.

Statistics

Your week: was the air good, and what did it cost?

Statistics reads the unit's history and answers four questions: how fresh the air was, how much the fans used compared with the 7 days before and with the same dates last year, why that changed, and what the app did.

At a glance

A one-word verdict, then Energy and Fresh air against the 7 days before or, until there are any, the same dates last year. Tap Open for the window.

The 7 days before

The verdict, Fresh air (CO2 up to 800 ppm), CO2 avg and Fan avg, then → now, and Energy. Green is better, amber worse. Below: the same dates last year.

Electricity and why

Icons split the change in electricity into three reasons: settings, people and weather. The fourth: heater kWh, counted by the app.

Settings

You changed a mode's speed or ran a different mode.

People

The unit added air because CO2 rose: more people at home, guests.

Weather and other

Humidity boosts, summer cooling, manual boosts.

How it works

  • The verdict. Better: energy down or fresh air up, nothing worse. Worse: the opposite. Mixed: one better, the other worse. Similar: both changed less than 2 %. Tap ? for How to read.
  • Electricity from fan speed. Fan power grows roughly with the cube of speed, so the change is shown in percent, not kWh.
  • Reads the settings from the history. For every hour it finds the mode's base speed and the CO2 limit that was in use, so changes made on the panel or in the Vallox cloud are counted correctly.
  • Splits the change. Base speed counts as Settings, the extra added for CO2 as People, everything else as Weather and other.
  • Heating season. When it is cold outside, it also shows heat loss per degree of cold, which takes the weather out of the comparison.
App activity · 7 days

What the automation did this week: hours with the fan lowered because nobody was home, hours of cooling with outdoor air and of keeping heat, Boost offers.

Change log · 7 days

Every change to the unit, newest first: by the App, by You in the app, or Unit - its panel or the Vallox cloud, noticed at the next reading.

Statistics · last year

Better or worse than a year ago, with the weather taken out.

The Vallox cloud keeps a year of your unit's history. The controlling device downloads it, keeps a short summary of every week and compares your last 7 days with the same dates last year. The other device receives the result.

On the Statistics page

The page and the STATISTICS card of the phone panel compare with the 7 days before. Last year shows there only until those 7 days exist; the bottom line always names both periods.

In the window

The second block: its own verdict, Fresh air, CO2 avg and Fan avg against last year, and Energy without the weather. Two icons below: a cloud with the change including the weather, and a thermometer with last year's average outdoor temperature.

Needs the cloud

The year of history comes only from the Vallox cloud, so the controlling device needs the account. Without it the block says so, and the comparison with the 7 days before still works.

How it works

  • The same dates. The same calendar dates a year ago: 8-14 November against 8-14 November last year. They are taken from the kept weekly summaries, within a day; without them, from the cloud's year while it still holds those days.
  • Weather taken out. The change in fan electricity is split as on the previous page; the weather part is removed, so the number shows what your settings and your home changed.
  • Kept on the device that calculates. The cloud keeps exactly 365 days. The weekly summaries stay on the controlling device, so the comparison keeps working when old days drop out of the cloud. After you switch devices, last year can take about 7 days to appear.

Requirements

Wear OS 4 or newer; the phone app needs Android 8 or newer. A Vallox unit with MyVallox. The phone app needs a free MyVallox cloud account; the watch can also work on your home network alone.

Privacy

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your account stays on your watch and phone, password encrypted, sent only to the Vallox cloud.

Price

Free to download. Pro (smart modes, Optimize, Statistics) is a one-time purchase with 14 days free to try. Settings shows the days left and Unlock Pro. When the trial ends one notice says so: Analyse turns into Unlock, Optimized opens the Pro window, and Statistics keep the last numbers with their date.

On your wrist without opening the app

Complications

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Customize, pick a slot and choose Wrist Control. Tap a complication to open the app.

The diamond is the unit's heat exchanger, drawn like on the unit itself. The white dot shows where the temperature is measured. Filter change shows days left, with a minus when overdue. After 3 h without new data a complication shows --.

Notifications

Temperaturebelow 18 or above 27 °C
Humiditybelow 25 or above 60 %
CO2above 800 ppm, again above 1200
Unit clockmore than 5 min off; tap to fix

One notice per level, a new one only when it gets worse; it disappears as soon as the value improves. The scale is the same as in the app, so you see how far out you are. Filters get a notice when a change is due. Temperature, humidity, CO2 and filter notices can be turned off in Settings → Alerts. The clock notice cannot: a wrong unit clock spoils the history that Optimize and Statistics rely on. Choose Off under Unit clock to silence it. A high CO2 or humidity notice opens the mode list, in Automatic the air screen with the Boost button. "Cooking or shower?" with Tap to Boost starts Boost on the watch; on the phone it opens the mode list, in Automatic the panel with the Boost button. The filter notice opens Filters. Of the connection notices, Password not accepted and No contact with the unit after 3 h without readings, while air alerts are paused, have no switch and come once. They disappear when the connection is back. On a phone with Android 13 or newer the app asks for permission to show notifications when the panel or Setup opens; if it was refused, Settings → Alerts offers Turn on.
